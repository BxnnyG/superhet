# Scanner

Scans a frequency range or a channel list, stops on a signal and carries on after a hang time.
Design: ADR-0025. Per device (`{dev}`); several devices scan independently.

## How it works

The scanner watches one *window* (one device centre) at a time. Every channel inside the window is
measured on the same spectrum frame, so a scan inside the visible span needs no retune; channels
outside are grouped into windows and the device is retuned between them (the current window is
visited first). A channel is a *hit* when its mean power over the channel width is at least
`threshold_db` above the noise floor (30th percentile of the usable bins), or at least
`threshold_dbfs` if given, in two consecutive frames after the `dwell_ms` of the window. The first
hit (priority channel first, then ascending frequency) stops the scan: the VFO moves to the
channel and the scanner *holds* while the signal stays within 3 dB of the threshold. When the
channel is let go the rest of the window is measured at once, then the scan moves on.

Resume rule (`resume`): `hang` (default) lets go `hang_ms` after the signal vanished, `timeout`
`timeout_ms` after the stop whatever the signal does, `hold` never (resume or skip from the user).
`priority.freq_hz` is checked every `interval_ms`: by visiting its window when it is elsewhere,
and from within a hold when it is in the same window (it then interrupts the hold).

Skip leaves the held channel (it is ignored for 30 s) or the current window. Lock out removes a
frequency from the scan until it is unlocked (kept in memory while the daemon runs).

While a scan runs it holds the tuning lock (holder kind `scanner`): other centre or rate changes
get `locked`. A user lock taken meanwhile keeps the scan in its current window (state
`restricted`; `blocked: "locked"` when nothing is left there). A plugin that claims the device
exclusively ends the scan (`stopped: "claimed"`); starting needs a free device.
The VFO that listens is `vfo` (default: the lowest id); its mode, frequency and bandwidth are set
at each stop, set a squelch on it to keep it silent between hits.

Limits: 2000 channels in a list, 20 000 generated by a range, 2000 lockouts; a channel must fit the
usable window (85 % of the sample rate).

## REST

`POST /api/v1/devices/{dev}/scanner/{action}`, operators and admins; same CSRF rules and error
shape as `/tune` (`400 invalid`, `404` unknown VFO or action, `409 locked`, `503`, `504`). Bodies
up to 256 KiB, `v` defaults to 1. The reply is `{"v":1,"ok":true,"state":{...}}`; the state
carries `scanner`. Actions:

- `start`: `{"vfo"?, "source": {"kind":"range","start_hz","stop_hz","step_hz","mode"?,"bandwidth_hz"?}
  | {"kind":"list","name"?,"channels":[{"freq_hz","mode"?,"bandwidth_hz"?,"label"?}]},
  "threshold_db"?=10 (1..60), "threshold_dbfs"?, "dwell_ms"?=150 (20..5000), "hang_ms"?=2000,
  "resume"?="hang"|"timeout"|"hold", "timeout_ms"?=10000, "priority"?:{"freq_hz","mode"?,
  "bandwidth_hz"?,"label"?,"interval_ms"?=5000}, "lockout_hz"?:[...]}`. A running scan is
  replaced. Without `mode` a channel gets the default mode of its band (as for a new VFO).
- `stop`, `pause`, `resume` (also lets go of a held channel), `skip`: `{"v":1}`.
- `lockout`: `{"freq_hz"?}` (default: the held channel). `unlock`: `{"freq_hz"}` or `{"all":true}`.

Bus: the same bodies on `sh.v1.dev.<dev>.ctl.scanner.<action>`; hits on
`sh.v1.dev.<dev>.scanner.hit` (JSON: `ts_ms, device, vfo, freq_hz, mode, bandwidth_hz, label,
level_dbfs, margin_db, priority, source, list`).

## State

`scanner` in the device state document: `state` (`idle|scanning|holding|paused|blocked`), `phase`
(`signal|hang` while holding), `vfo`, `pass`, `window` (`index,count,center_hz,low_hz,high_hz`),
`current` (the held channel with `level_dbfs`, `margin_db`), `blocked`, `restricted`, `stopped`
(why the last scan ended: `stopped`, `claimed`, `vfo_removed`, `shutdown`), `started_ms`, `config`
(source and settings), `lockouts`, `hits` (newest first, at most 25). Clients must tolerate it
being absent.

## History

Every hit is stored in the decoder history (`docs/api-decoder-events.md`) with `source`
`scanner`, kind `scanner.hit`, the VFO, frequency and mode; `value` reads
`PMR 1 (446.00625 MHz) +43 dB`. The NATS user `worker` needs subscribe rights on
`sh.v1.dev.*.scanner.hit` (in `deploy/nats/nats.conf`).

## Web

Inspector tab *Scanner* (all roles see it; operators and admins control it): source (range,
bookmarks with a group or those marked for scanning, channel plan), settings, status with the held
channel, Start/Stop, Pause/Resume, Skip, Lock out, lockout list and the hit list (click tunes the
active VFO). Keys: `G` start/stop (repeats the last scan), `P` pause/resume, `K` skip, `L` lock
out the held channel. The settings and the last scan are remembered in the browser.
